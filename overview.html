Smart farming tips

General guidance — not a farm-specific diagnosis
Check soil moisture

Before irrigation, inspect soil moisture at root depth and consider the crop's growth stage.

Monitor changing weather

Review local forecasts before spraying or applying fertilizers.

Scout your crops

Inspect leaves and field edges regularly for early signs of pests or stress.

Keep field records

Record planting, irrigation, and treatment dates to support better planning.
