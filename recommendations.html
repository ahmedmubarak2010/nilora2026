3 guidance cards · Evidence first
Data needed

Irrigation planning

A safe irrigation schedule needs crop type, growth stage, soil properties, recent weather, and a reliable water source.

What information is missing?
Add your field boundary, crop and planting date. Soil texture and irrigation method improve the assessment. NILORA will not invent water volumes or watering times.
Lab data needed

Soil & nutrition

Fertilizer decisions should be based on soil analysis, crop needs, growth stage, and local agricultural guidance.

What information is missing?
A recent soil test (such as pH, EC and available nutrients), crop variety and planting date are needed before discussing a nutrient plan. Do not apply fertilizer based on this placeholder.
Imagery not checked

Satellite crop health

Vegetation indices can help identify areas for inspection, but they do not diagnose a disease or prove a cause on their own.

How is this assessed?
A valid assessment needs a field boundary, cloud-screened imagery, sensor and acquisition date, crop type and growth stage. Compare multiple dates and confirm unusual patterns with a field visit.
