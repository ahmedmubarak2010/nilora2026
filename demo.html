01 · The challenge

Making complex data useful in the field

Farmers and agricultural professionals need to understand changing environmental conditions, but Earth observation and climate information can be difficult to find, interpret, and turn into everyday decisions.

Scattered informationUseful climate and satellite resources are spread across different platforms.
Technical barriersRaw data and specialist tools can be difficult for non-technical users.
