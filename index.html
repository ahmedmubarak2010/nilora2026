About NILORA

Technology that makes agricultural information easier to understand.

We are building a practical way to bring field records, satellite-derived indicators and climate context into one place—without presenting estimates as ground truth.

For farmers

A simpler view of farm and field information, with clear explanations and practical next steps. The experience is being designed to stay accessible and easy to use.

For agronomists

A more detailed workspace to review field context, compare available indicators and support recommendations with professional judgement.

Our approach

From field information to a clearer picture.

The intended workflow is simple. Recommendations depend on the quality and availability of data and should be reviewed by an agricultural specialist.

01

Add a field

Record the farm and field location, crop details and any available observations.

02

Review available data

Explore relevant satellite and climate indicators, with their dates, sources and limitations.

03

Make an informed plan

Use the available context with agronomist review before making operational decisions.

Data transparency: Satellite and gridded climate products are estimates at their own spatial and temporal resolutions. They are not a substitute for in-field measurements, laboratory tests or professional diagnosis.
